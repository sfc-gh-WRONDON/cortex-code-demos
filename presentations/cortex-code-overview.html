The pieces, and where they sit

•

Your prompt: in the moment, right at the Turn. Says what you want now.

•

Skill: loaded ahead of the Turn. Durable knowledge and procedure, but easily buried under intervening work.

•

Hook: fires on an event around the Turn. Injects a nudge, or gates an action.

•

MCP: wraps a tool's request and its response. Reshapes what the agent sends, and what it gets back.

•

Plugin: packages the above so other people can adopt it.

Two of these sit right on the Turn: hooks and MCP.
That is where the leverage lives.
